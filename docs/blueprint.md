# **App Name**: Abyssal Conclave

## Core Features:

- Member Registration & Login: Secure user registration and authentication allowing new members to join and existing ones to log in to the community.
- Personalized Aethel: Users can customize their unique character profile, including a profile picture, display name, and a short, thematic bio, enhancing their MMORPG identity.
- Grand Hall Chat: A central, real-time chat room for all online members to interact and communicate in a persistent public space.
- Guild Roster: A dynamic list displaying all members currently logged in and active on the platform, fostering a sense of immediate community presence.
- Shadowed Chronicle AI: An AI-powered tool that generates snippets of dark fantasy lore or character backstories based on user-inputted keywords and themes, to inspire member profiles or forum posts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep, smoldering red (#BF0A23) for an intense, dominant tone that reflects the dark fantasy theme.
- Background color: A rich, subtle reddish-charcoal gray (#221B1C), providing a somber and immersive backdrop.
- Accent color: A desaturated, metallic-like mauve (#BCB3B8), offering a glint of polished stone or aged silver for highlights and interactive elements, analogous to the primary hue.
- Headline font: 'Playfair' (serif) for a dramatic, high-contrast, elegant aesthetic that suits the MMORPG theme. Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clear readability in chat messages and profile details.
- Utilize dark fantasy-themed icons and glyphs (e.g., stylized skulls, weapons, ancient runes) that are evocative of MMORPG user interfaces like Diablo 4 and World of Warcraft, using line art and muted metallic gradients.
- Employ a modular, layered layout with dark panels and clear separation between sections, mimicking in-game UI screens for an immersive user experience. Ensure elements appear 'etched' or 'chiseled' from dark stone.
- Incorporate subtle animations for hover states, button presses, and transitions, such as faint 'glow' effects around interactive elements in the primary red or accent metallic hues, or gentle 'smoke' wisps on background changes.
